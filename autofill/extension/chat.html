Teach the extension

Type a fact in plain English — “my address changed to Pune”, “now I have 3 years of experience”, “my notice period is 60 days”. The extension works out which part of your profile that touches, shows you exactly what it wants to change, and changes nothing until you press Apply. Everything is stored on this device only, and survives a browser or device restart.

Enter sends · Shift+Enter starts a new line · nothing is saved until you press Apply

What I’ve learned

Answers you gave the extension when it asked about a form question it could not work out on its own. These are reused automatically the next time the same question appears. Press Forget on anything that is wrong or out of date.
